# Ferry
Port Hardy -> Prince Rupert 隔一天一班(一周是周六一周是周日)
Prince Rupert -> Haida Gwaii 班次密集
去程初始日確定坐 ferry, 回程開車

### Prince Rupert → Port Hardy

- March/April/May/Oct/Nov/Dec
    - Every Friday and every other Tuesday
    - Tue: 6:30pm ~ 1pm
    - Fri: 11am~9am
- June/July/August/Sep
    - Every other day
    - 7:30am ~11:50pm


### Port Hardy → Prince Rupert
- March/April/May/Oct/Nov/Dec
    - Every Saturday and every other Wednesday
    - Sat 6pm ~ 4pm
    - Wed 4:30pm ~ 11am
- June/July/August/Sep
    - Every other day
    - 7:30am ~11:50pm
